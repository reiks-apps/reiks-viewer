Play forward and backward get three step sizes, and holding the key starts one frame at a time.

## Download

- **ReiksViewer-Setup-1.6.0.exe**: the installer. Installing for yourself needs no administrator rights.
- **ReiksViewer-1.6.0-portable.zip**: no install; unzip anywhere and run `ReiksViewer.exe`.

`ReiksViewer-Setup.exe` and `ReiksViewer-portable.zip` are the same downloads without the version in the name. To update, run the new installer over the old one.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not digitally signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **Three step sizes for play forward and backward.** Press `Ctrl` on its own to switch between one frame, 10 seconds and 2% of the video, and once more to go back to frames. A short note over the video and a label on the two step buttons show which one is on. `Ctrl` + `Left` / `Right` (5 seconds) and `Ctrl` + `P` work as before.
- **Holding starts one frame at a time.** Holding `X` or `Z`, or a step button, used to start at normal playback speed; now it starts one frame at a time and gets faster the longer you hold, as before.
- **Jumps keep the video playing.** A 10-second or 2% jump leaves a playing video playing; while the key is held it pauses, and it carries on when you let go. Stepping frames pauses the video, as before.
